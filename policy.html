Policy & Terms

Last updated: 24 July 2026

Privacy Policy

ConsoleHaven collects only the information needed to operate your account and process your orders: your username, email address, delivery address and order history. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

Passwords are never stored in readable form. All account credentials are protected using secure hashing before being written to our database.

Shipping & Delivery

Standard delivery takes five to seven business days and is free on orders over $50. Express delivery arrives within two to three business days for $9.99. Store pickup is available within 24 hours of ordering.

Returns & Refunds

Unopened products may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Opened software and digital download codes cannot be returned once redeemed, except where the product is faulty.

To start a return, contact our support team with your order number. Refunds are processed to the original payment method within five business days of us receiving the returned item.
