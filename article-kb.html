Top 5 Mechanical Keyboards for Competitive E-sports

#Esports #Gear #Hardware

In competitive e-sports, every millisecond counts. Your brain might react fast enough, but if your hardware introduces latency, you've already lost the duel. Today, we are breaking down the fastest mechanical keyboards used by the pros.

Optical vs. Mechanical Switches

Traditional mechanical switches rely on physical metal contacts, which require a "debounce delay" to prevent accidental double-clicks. Optical switches, however, use a beam of light to register the keystroke, effectively reducing input delay to zero.

"Upgrading to a rapid-trigger magnetic switch keyboard shaved 15ms off my reaction time in Valorant. It feels like cheating, but it's just physics."
