Product Description

A vivid seven inch OLED screen brings handheld play to life with richer colours and deeper contrast than the standard model. The wide adjustable stand makes tabletop mode genuinely practical, and the dock now includes a wired LAN port for stable online play.

Three play modes in one system: dock it to play on the television, flip out the stand for tabletop sessions with a friend, or slide on the Joy-Con controllers and take the whole console with you.
